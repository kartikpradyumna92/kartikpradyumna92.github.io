{% include section-head.html num="01" label="About" id="about" title="A full-stack data scientist, from the data model to the decision." %}

Where I started

I was the first Data Analyst at Skupos, an early-stage startup. After setting up the company’s KPIs and source-of-truth tables, I built and scaled a Prophet sales-forecasting model for high-volume SKUs across 150+ convenience stores. It automated inventory decisions and reduced over-deliveries and stockouts, with a forecast-vs-actual dashboard tracking how well it performed.

What I do now

At Teladoc Health since 2020, I work on member engagement for weight management and diabetes prevention programs. I build the data layer too: dbt models on member and clinical data, with pipelines orchestrated as Airflow DAGs. My favorite problems sit at a decision point: a launch that needs an honest read, a funnel drop-off to fix, an at-risk account that needs a plan. I turn each into a testable question, use the design the data supports (a randomized test when possible, difference-in-differences when not), and say plainly what the result does and doesn’t show.

What I’m looking for

Data Scientist roles across experimentation, causal inference, product analytics and data modeling, on teams where analysis shapes what gets built.

Outside work I’m a passionate photographer, which led me to build Hue & Seek (opens in a new tab), an Android app for single-color photo walks.
